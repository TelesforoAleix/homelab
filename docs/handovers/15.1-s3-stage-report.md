## Findings

1. **A deleted gateway key answers HTTP 400 `invalid_request_error`, not 401.** The helper maps
   only 401 to `provider_error` (released, $0) and 429 to `exhausted`; every other non-2xx after
   egress is settled at the reserved maximum. That is the right default for money, and it means
   the fail-closed ledger class now has two members. Options for S4/15: map the gateway's
   documented `invalid_request_error` + 400 to a release when `usage` is absent, or leave it — the
   overshoot is bounded by one reservation.
2. **The executor cannot fire a paying call.** Claude Code's auto-mode permission layer refused
   the call-4 `curl` as a real-world transaction. Nothing was worked around: the call was staged as
   `p151-s3-call4.sh` with its own one-attempt marker and the owner ran
   `ssh homelab-agent bash /tmp/homelab-agent/p151-s3-call4.sh` from the Mac. For ADR-049's
   convention: **a step that spends is OWNER-fired even when it is AGENT-runnable**; the agent
   stages and reads. Worth a sentence in the 13.1 guide at S4.
3. **`X-Homelab-Client` is 32 characters at most.** The runbook's first call-4 label was 36; the
   endpoint refused it (4x). Fixed in the runbook.
4. **Pasting into a wrapped terminal is an attempt hazard.** One heredoc ran on the wrong machine,
   one lost its `EOF`, one one-liner split mid-header (4y). Every remaining OWNER command became a
   staged script with one short invocation. Keep that shape.
5. **The draft had already been run** (16:32–16:44Z) with a wrong role name (`utility-unattended`
   → P2). The rewrite found it in the journals before spending anything; the two locally refused
   hour calls were accepted from the audit lines instead of repeated.
6. **Credit balance** reported by the owner: $15 ($5 gifted promo) — balance, not spend; for
   `costs.md` context only.
